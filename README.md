# Adjektivendungen — German adjective endings practice

Practice German adjective endings in the **Nominativ**, **Akkusativ** and **Dativ**, from easiest to hardest, with instant correction and an explanation for every answer.

The interface is available in **Arabic, French and English**. Use the buttons at the top of the page to switch.

## Contents

92 exercises in 7 levels:

| # | Level | Exercises | Type |
|---|-------|-----------|------|
| 1 | Nominativ | 13 | Choose the ending |
| 2 | Akkusativ | 13 | Choose the ending |
| 3 | Dativ | 13 | Choose the ending |
| 4 | Find the case | 12 | Nominativ, Akkusativ or Dativ? |
| 5 | Mixed | 15 | Prepositions and verbs that take Akkusativ or Dativ |
| 6 | Writing | 14 | Type the article and the adjective |
| 7 | Right or wrong? | 12 | Spot the mistake |

## Features

- Instant correction, with the explanation in the chosen language
- A list of your mistakes at the end of each level, and a button to redo only those questions
- A shuffle option for the questions
- Your best score on each level is saved in the browser
- Review tables for the three cases, plus the key rules
- Works on phones, with light and dark mode
- Keyboard shortcuts: number keys choose an option, Enter moves to the next question

## Run it

It's one file with no dependencies. Open `index.html` in any browser.

To put it online, turn on **GitHub Pages** in the repository's settings (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root).
